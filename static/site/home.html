    Built on PayPal, end to end.

    Every pound in ScopePay moves through PayPal. Follow one grocery order from the buyer's wallet to the shop's account.

    1. 1

      Link PayPal once

      PayPal Vault

      The buyer approves PayPal a single time. ScopePay never holds their password.

    2. 2

      Charge without a popup

      Orders v2 with vault_id

      When a code passes its rules, the saved PayPal is charged instantly, with the basket's line items attached.

    3. 3

      Pay the shop

      Payouts

    4. 4

      Or hold it in escrow

      PYUSD on Sepolia

      Optional: settle in PayPal USD inside a refund window, so an agent's mistake can be undone.

    Why PayPal?It's already the buyer's wallet, the merchant's checkout and the place disputes get settled. That makes it the one network that can carry an agent's instructions from start to finish.

    Your rules, checked on every payment.

    Each code carries its own limits. The agent proposes a purchase; ScopePay's rules decide, before any money moves.

    Spending cap

    £60 for the week's groceries, tracked across every purchase on the code.

    Merchant lock

    A Greenleaf code is declined at any other shop, even one that takes PayPal.

    Expiry

    From 15 minutes to 7 days. After that the code is worthless.

    Single or multi-use

    One purchase and done, or several until the cap runs out.

    Revoke anytime

    Cancel a code from your wallet and the next payment attempt is refused.

    No credentials shared

    Codes are shown once and stored only as a hash. Your PayPal details stay with PayPal.

    Proof for every payment, approved or not.

    Each step becomes a record chained to the one before it, so history can't be quietly edited. Hand it to the shop, to PayPal or to an arbiter if a purchase is disputed.
