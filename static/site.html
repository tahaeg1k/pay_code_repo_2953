Your rules, enforced

All the control. None of the anxiety.

Set the rules once. ScopePay checks every one of them before PayPal moves a penny.

💷

Spending cap

£60 means £60, across one payment or ten.

🏪

Merchant lock

Only usable at the shop you chose. Anywhere else, declined.

⏱️

Auto-expiry

15 minutes to 7 days, then the code is dead.

1️⃣

Single or multi-use

One purchase, or many until the cap is spent.

🛑

Revoke anytime

One tap and the agent's spending power is gone.

🔒

No credential sharing

Your PayPal stays in PayPal's vault. Agents only hold a code.

Evidence trail

A payment can clear and still be wrong.

So every decision is written down: the rules you set, the basket, each check that passed or failed, and PayPal's order and capture IDs. Records are hash-chained, so history can't be quietly edited.

When something goes wrong, the merchant, PayPal and you are looking at the same facts.
